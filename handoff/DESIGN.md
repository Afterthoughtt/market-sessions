# Market Sessions — design spec

The product and visual spec for the shipped app. The sections below describe the popover, type and colour; "Owner decisions" amends them and wins on conflict.

## Files
- popover.html — the popover's visual baseline at 340pt, inline-styled, no dependencies (a fixed snapshot, Thu 6:09 PM PDT). Its menu-bar label is out of date; the label is specified under Menu bar label below.

## Popover
- Width 340pt. Height = content. Material and corners are the system window's (`MenuBarExtra` `.window` style, Liquid Glass with the kit's 20pt popover corners on macOS 27); the app draws no material, card or ring of its own. The only colour is the state-coloured section header text.
- Padding 14pt sides/top, 4pt bottom.
- Header: title "Market Sessions" 15 semibold; subtitle = weekday, 13 secondary. Right column: "Daily Close in 22h 51m" 13 secondary (countdown value semibold primary, tabular) on the weekday's baseline; only when Settings overrides the zone, "Time · EST" 11 secondary above it (owner decision 2026-09-29: the system zone needs no label). Daily Close's tooltip: "Time left until the daily close at midnight UTC".
- Sections: Open, Pre-Market, After Hours, Holiday, Closed (empty ones omitted). Section header: the state name 11 semibold in the state colour (owner decision 2026-09-30); 16pt above, 2pt below; VoiceOver treats it, and the Closures and Upcoming Events headers, as a heading. No hairlines between rows: section headers and whitespace do the grouping (owner decision 2026-09-29).
- Market row, 40pt (owner decision 2026-09-30, design C "time left first"): name 13 primary over the next change 11 secondary tabular ("Closes 11:30 PM", "Opens Wed 6:30 AM", "Reopens 3:00 PM" during a lunch recess or CME maintenance); right, the time until that change as the main value: while Open "9m left" (13 semibold primary + 11 secondary "left"), otherwise "in 7h 9m" (11 secondary "in" + 13 regular, primary for Pre-Market and After Hours, secondary for Holiday and Closed). Open rows add a bar under the row (owner decision 2026-10-22, restoring the 2026-09-29 bar): the kit's Small determinate bar, 6pt tall and fully rounded, track 8% black (8% white in dark), fill the secondary label colour, filling from the start of the uninterrupted open run (`ResolvedSession.activeStart`; it restarts after a lunch recess or maintenance, not at a contiguous auction) toward the close shown, never narrower than a 6pt dot once started; 6pt below it. Other rows have no bar and no row tint. Sort each section by soonest change. The value is 13pt, the kit's detail-label size (owner decision 2026-09-30, replacing 17pt, which outsized the 15pt title).
- Each selected market appears in exactly one section.
- Upcoming Events: header "Upcoming Events"; always next 4, no disclosure. Row 24pt: name 13 primary, right 13 secondary date "Fri, Sep 4"; inside 24h the right side becomes "Tomorrow 7:00 AM" in semibold primary. Short names (Nonfarm Payrolls, CPI, FOMC Decision…). Which events to show is a Settings preference.
- Footer: full-width hairline (system separator colour, bleeds to popover edge) 5pt below the last row (the kit's 11pt menu separator), then one 32pt row "Settings…" 13 medium primary: the kit's 24pt menu item (its hover highlight is 24pt tall) with 4pt above and below, so the last item clears the popover's rounded bottom. On hover it takes the kit's menu-item highlight: accent fill, white label, 8pt corners (owner decision 2026-09-30); the same highlight shows while pressed and under keyboard focus. Opens the Settings window (see Settings). No other controls in the popover.
- Warning line: one 11pt secondary line above the Settings hairline, only in the cases listed under Popover › Footer below.

## Type
macOS text styles (HIG Typography, macOS built-in styles), so sizes come from the system rather than the code: Title 3 Semibold (15) for the popover title · Body (13) for names, trailing times, header readouts and the Settings… row (Medium) · Subheadline (11) for section headers (Semibold), row subtitles and footnote lines. The kit's form and menu detail labels are 13pt (owner found 10–11pt too small, 2026-09-29). Settings form rows keep fixed 13/11pt Medium from the kit's Forms page, since no text style is Medium at those sizes. Weights in use: Regular, Medium, Semibold. Tabular figures (`.monospacedDigit()`) on every time and countdown.

## Colour
Primary text, secondary text and the hairline are the system label, secondary label and separator colours, so they follow Increase Contrast (owner decision 2026-09-30, replacing fixed opacities). Secondary label is about 3.9:1 in light mode, under 4.5:1; kept to match system popovers, since Increase Contrast darkens it (owner decision 2026-10-30). The kit's label tokens give their values: black at 0.85 / 0.50 in light, white at 0.85 / 0.55 in dark. State colours follow TradingView's status mapping: Open green, Pre-Market orange, After Hours blue, Holiday and Closed secondary. They are named colours in `Resources/Assets.xcassets` (`OpenText`, `PreMarketText`, `AfterHoursText`), the kit's accents adjusted to read as 11pt text on the popover (owner decisions 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-29). Plain system colours fall to 2.0–3.3:1 in light mode, so they are not used.

## Owner decisions (amend the sections above; these win on conflict)

### Sessions (six available, all shown by default)

| Code | Name | Canonical zone | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `NY` | New York | `America/New_York` | 9:30–16:00 weekdays; pre-market 4:00–9:30, post-market 16:00–20:00 |
| `CME` | CME Futures | `America/Chicago` | Equity-index session: Sun–Fri 17:00–16:00 next day; Mon–Thu 16:00–17:00 break; no estimate marker |
| `LDN` | London | `Europe/London` | 8:00–16:30 weekdays |
| `TYO` | Tokyo | `Asia/Tokyo` | 9:00–11:30, 12:30–15:30; lunch recess |
| `HKG` | Hong Kong | `Asia/Hong_Kong` | 9:30–12:00, 13:00–16:00; recess; closing auction 16:00–16:10 (outer bound) |
| `SHG` | Shanghai | `Asia/Shanghai` | 9:30–11:30, 13:00–14:57; recess; closing auction 14:57–15:00 |

Schedules are defined in canonical IANA zones. Display time defaults to the system zone; Settings can override it. Display-zone changes never move schedule instants or the UTC daily close. Holidays and early closes come from the bundled exceptions file (see `MarketSessions/Resources/README.md`): holiday occurrences are dropped, sessions straddling an early close are clamped, same-day remnants after it are dropped, and overnight re-opens (CME's evening session after a holiday halt) are kept.

### States and appearance

Five market states, TradingView's statuses without Overnight (no bundled market trades overnight): **Open, Pre-Market, After Hours, Holiday, Closed** (owner decision 2026-09-30, replacing Break). Trading and contiguous auctions resolve to Open; pre-open windows to Pre-Market (New York 4:00–9:30 ET early trading; London 7:50–8:00 opening auction call; Tokyo 8:00–9:00 order acceptance; Hong Kong 9:00–9:30 pre-opening auction; Shanghai 9:15–9:30 opening call auction plus the gap to continuous trading) and New York's 16:00–20:00 ET late session to After Hours (exchange-published hours, verified 2026-09-05, see `finance-market-sessions-reference.md`). A lunch recess or CME's maintenance hour is Closed, reopening at the next session (TradingView has no pause status). A closed market whose canonical date is a bundled holiday is Holiday, and so is one whose recurring session would be trading now but for a holiday (CME's evening session before a closure). Only Open counts as active. Transition readouts say Opens/Closes/Reopens with the scheduled boundary and no estimate marker. Colours: see Colour above.

### Popover (340 wide, height fits content)

A popover, not a menu (owner decision 2026-10-29): the HIG's menu bar extras guidance allows a popover when the content is too complex for a menu, and two-line rows, right-aligned times, state-coloured section headers and progress bars do not fit menu items.

1. Header — title and local weekday; `Time · <zone>` only for a Settings override; UTC "Daily Close in Xh Ym".
2. Open — active markets ordered by nearest close: close time and time left. Omitted when no market is open (owner decision 2026-10-30), so a green header always means trading.
3. Pre-Market, After Hours, Holiday, then Closed — ordered by nearest change: open time and time until it, even when an earlier Pre-Market or After Hours edge comes first; the section header names the current state (owner decision 2026-10-30). Omit empty sections. Each selected market appears once. Rows are non-interactive.
4. Closures — holidays and early closes for the selected markets in each market's next seven days, not counting today (today's holiday is the Holiday section; today's early close is on its row). Same 24pt row style as Upcoming Events, header "Closures" 11 semibold secondary; rows "New York · Thanksgiving" or "New York · closes 1:00 PM", right "Thu, Nov 26". A holiday shows its date in the market's own zone; an early close shows its time and date in the display zone. Sorted by the day shown, hidden when there are none. A close already on an Open row is not repeated. Any row whose close is a bundled early close appends ", early" to its close time ("Closes Thu 1:00 PM, early"), including in its accessibility label. Owner decision 2026-10-29 (option B). Every closure in the week shows, uncapped (owner decision 2026-10-30: Christmas week with all six markets runs to nine rows rather than hiding Christmas Day). An early close's tooltip and accessibility label add the market's own date and time when its zone differs from the display zone ("(Dec 24, 2026 at 12:00 PM in Hong Kong)").
5. Upcoming Events — the next four of the selected kinds, no disclosure. Compact names (U.S. Jobs Report, FOMC Decision); full description and exact local date/time in tooltips and accessibility labels. Preserve per-event titles (Jackson Hole keynote). Inside 24 hours emphasize Today/Tomorrow + local time; while live show Live. BOJ carries no approximation marker (owner decision 2026-09-29); its stored 12:00 JST is noted only in the Settings tooltip. When nothing selected remains, say the bundled schedule has none. Bundled files cover whole years, so a kind is covered through Dec 31 of its last bundled year; when a selected category's earliest-ending selected kind runs out before the last shown row, one 11pt secondary line says so ("U.S. release dates bundled through Dec 31, 2026"). Fed Chair appearances have no published calendar and never count; Settings rows past their category's coverage (the last date of its earliest-ending kind) read "Not in bundled schedule".
6. Footer — holiday-data warning only when unavailable or expired, or when a shown open/close or the Closures week falls after the data ends ("Holiday data ends Dec 31, 2026; later times assume regular hours"), then a full-width Settings row (⌘,) with the kit's menu-item hover.

### Menu bar label

A fixed glyph that does not convey market state (owner decision 2026-10-02); the popover carries state and times. The glyph is three solid candlesticks rising left to right, the app icon's motif drawn heavier so it matches Wi‑Fi's weight (owner pick "Blue Glass", 2026-10-29). Geometry in a 16pt box, y from the top: bodies 4.2pt wide with corners at 30% of the width, centred at x 2.5, 8 and 13.5; bodies span 7–13.4, 4.8–11.2 and 2.6–9pt; 1.5pt wicks with round caps run 1.6pt past each body end. A template image so the system tints it. Tooltip and accessibility label: the next-to-change session's full name, status, and "Closes 1:00 PM", or "Market Sessions — choose markets in Settings" with no markets selected.

### App icon

`MarketSessions/Resources/AppIcon.icon`, an Icon Composer document (owner pick "Blue Glass", 2026-10-29): three white candlesticks rising on an automatic gradient from #1E7BF2, one glass layer per candle, flat SVG fills so Icon Composer supplies the glass, light and shadow. Candles on one grid in the 1024 canvas: bodies 168 wide with 50 corners, wicks 36 wide, centres 277, 512 and 747. In dark mode macOS drops the background and tints the candles blue. Edit it in Icon Composer (Xcode › Open Developer Tool); `ictool` inside Icon Composer.app renders previews from the command line.

### Settings

Native Settings scene, four tabs, each sized to its content with no scrolling (owner decision 2026-09-30; measured at 520 wide: General 455, Markets 362, Events 785, Notifications 227pt) (General `gearshape`, Markets `globe.badge.clock`, Events `calendar.badge.clock`, Notifications `bell`; both badge symbols are available from macOS 26.0). Error subtitles name the cause and the fix. Rows follow the kit form pattern: 13pt Medium title, 11pt Medium secondary subtitle, trailing control or detail label aligned to the title line (the grouped Form's default; decision 2026-09-29); explanatory text lives in subtitles and section footers, not loose rows. General: Time Zone pop-up (North American zones plus UTC, stored as IANA identifiers, System by default, live zone name and offset in the subtitle), Holiday Coverage detail row; Update Dates Weekly switch whose subtitle gives the last update, or the site that failed and that it retries in a day, with a footer naming the sources and BEA's required notice ("This product uses the Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) Data API but is not endorsed or certified by BEA."); then Launch at Login and Quit. Markets: one row per session with a Notify checkbox, then the switch that shows it in the popover and menu bar (owner decision 2026-09-30: each market and event kind appears once in Settings). Events: U.S. Releases and Central Banks groups with the same Notify checkbox and Show switch per kind, each row's subtitle showing its next bundled date, footer stating the coverage per group. Selections apply immediately and persist in `MarketPreferences`. Turning off every market is allowed; turning off every event kind hides the section. Notifications: Lead Time pop-up (At the time, 5, 15, 30 minutes before) and the test-notification or permission row; the footer points to the Notify checkboxes. Notify is off by default and independent of Show, so a hidden market can still notify. While macOS has notifications turned off, the Markets and Events footers say so. A market notifies at the first open and final close of its trading day in the canonical zone (lunch recesses and CME's daily maintenance gap stay silent, holidays and early closes already applied); an event notifies at its scheduled start. Notifications are local `UNUserNotificationCenter` requests planned deterministically from the bundled schedule, authorization is requested on the first toggle, and a denied state shows one row linking to System Settings › Notifications. Body text uses the display zone.

### Non-features

No dependencies, telemetry or accounts. Network use is only the weekly event-date update (owner decision 2026-10-29, on by default): plain GETs of the Federal Reserve FOMC calendar and monthly calendar pages (meetings, minutes), BEA's `release_dates.json` (PCE), Census's retail sales schedule, the ECB meeting calendar and the BOJ meeting schedule. A source that fails or parses as too few dates keeps its earlier or bundled dates; fetched dates replace bundled ones only within the date range the source returned. BLS (CPI, jobs, PPI) blocks apps and GDP advance dates have no labelled machine-readable source, so those stay bundled; market holidays stay bundled because CME blocks apps and NYSE, LSE and HKEX terms forbid automated access. Excluded event kinds: jobless claims, ISM, JOLTS, consumer confidence, options expiries.

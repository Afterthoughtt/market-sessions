Four meanings, four colors
GreenContinuous trading.
PurpleAuction — orders match, but not continuously.
OrangePaused: lunch recess, or CME maintenance.
GreyClosed.
Merging both auctions onto one color collapsed six states to five, which is the honest count. It also resolved what was wrong before: Shanghai's auction was green and Hong Kong's orange, which encoded a real difference in a way nobody would ever decode. Purple says "auction"; the state column carries the rest.
CME, without a tooltip to hide in: its 2:00 PM close is a one-hour maintenance break, not a real close, so the row says Breaks — which is both shorter and truer than Closes. The ≈ carries the approximate settlement timing on the one row it applies to. Hong Kong's auction caveat is dropped.
One thing to watch in the menu bar: a filling ring and a draining ring look identical at 14 px. In 7c the closed state carries the ring at 62% opacity so the two read apart — the dimming, not the direction, is what tells you nothing is trading.
